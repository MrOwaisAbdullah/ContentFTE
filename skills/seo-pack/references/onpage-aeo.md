# On-Page SEO + AEO

## Title & H1
- Keyword at the **start** of title tag and H1. Title ≤ 60 chars, H1 = exact user intent (not clever).
- **Why:** Front-loaded keywords still win click-through and ranking for head terms.

## First sentence answers intent
- Open the article body with the direct answer — no throat-clearing intro.
- **Why:** Satisfies the query instantly (rankings) and gives AI systems a quotable lead.

## TL;DR block (40–60 words, right after H1)
- 2–3 sentence summary answering the query. Associated with **~+33% conversion** lift on BOFU pages.
- **Why:** Featured-snippet and AI-Overview bait; also the block users actually read.

## Question-form H2s with direct answers
- Turn sub-intents into questions as H2s; answer in 2–4 sentences immediately under each.
- **Why:** Matches PAA and voice/AI query format; each H2 is a standalone citable unit.

## FAQ block (4–8 Q&As) + FAQPage schema
- Keep on-page FAQ for UX and schema — but **don't rely on accordions alone**: high-value questions get dedicated pages (see coverage-multiplication).
- **Rule:** 4–8 questions, 40–60 word answers, valid FAQPage JSON-LD.

## Definition sentences
- Include at least one "X is…" sentence per key term.
- **Why:** Definition-style sentences are the most-extracted format for snippets and AI answers.

## Length matches intent
- Buyer-intent pages: short wins (400–500 words). Informational: as long as the gaps require — but **no 3,000-word fluff**.
- **Rule:** Cut anything that doesn't serve a listed sub-intent or missed-SERP-gap.
