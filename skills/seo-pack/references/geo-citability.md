# GEO / Citability

## Question-based citability scoring
- **Tactic:** Score each passage: is it self-contained? attributed? concise enough to quote? Aligned with Google's AI Optimization Guide.
- **Why:** LLMs cite passages, not pages. Uncitable passages = uncited page.
- **Rule:** New eval sub-score. Revision agent rewrites low-citability passages until they pass.

## Quotable stat blocks
- **Tactic:** Put key statistics in standalone sentences with the number first: "73% of…". One fact per sentence.
- **Why:** Numbers-first, single-fact sentences are the unit AI systems lift verbatim.

## "Best X for Y" self-first listicles
- **Tactic:** Publish well-researched "Best X for Y" pages where the client ranks first — a small fraction of total output. Must be neutral-toned, link competitors, stay factual and sourced.
- **Why:** "Best X" listicles are among the most-cited page types by AI assistants.
- **Rule:** Kept current — **recency is a citation factor**. Stale listicles lose citations first.

## Win-announcement posts
- **Tactic:** Publish client wins, case results, milestones as news-style posts.
- **Why:** Fresh, factual, entity-rich content that both search and LLMs pick up.

## Press releases (keyword discipline)
- **Tactic:** Keyword in the release title, subhead, and opening paragraph. Distribute via wire (e.g. AB Newswire, $6–80 tiers).
- **Why:** Press releases earn both search visibility and LLM citations for entity queries.

## Query fan-out pages
- **Tactic:** Build pages for the sub-queries LLMs fan out into (from brief research), not just the head query.
- **Why:** You win the citation by being the answer to the question the AI actually asks.

## Google Preferred Sources + Bing AI Performance
- **Tactic:** Enroll in Google Preferred Sources where eligible; mine Bing AI Performance data for citation gaps (queries where competitors get cited and you don't).
- **Why:** Shows exactly where your citability is losing.

## AI share-of-voice tracking
- **Tactic:** Monthly, prompt ChatGPT / Gemini / Perplexity / AI Overviews / AI Mode with category questions; record brand-mention share over time.
- **Why:** The GEO equivalent of rank tracking — the metric retainer clients actually care about.
