# Local SEO

## GBP ↔ website consistency
- Name, address, phone, hours, categories, services must match **exactly** between Google Business Profile and website.
- **Why:** The website is the verification database the Knowledge Graph checks GBP claims against. Mismatches suppress local rankings and AI recommendations.

## GBP changes trigger recrawls
- Any GBP edit (hours, services, photos) should trigger a full-site recrawl request.
- **Why:** Google re-verifies the entity after profile changes; a fresh crawl confirms consistency fast.

## Reviews on-site
- Surface real Google reviews on service/location pages (consideration stage).
- **Why:** Reviews are a ranking factor and the trust signal buyers check before contacting.

## Monitor fake AI reviews
- Watch for AI-generated fake reviews (competitor attacks or "helpful" spam). Report and document.
- **Why:** Fake review patterns can trigger profile penalties; early detection limits damage.

## Cheap local SERP checks
- Track local pack positions via API (DataForSEO/OpenSEO) on a schedule — don't rely on manual checks.
- **Why:** Local SERPs vary by location; automated grid checks catch what a single manual search misses.
