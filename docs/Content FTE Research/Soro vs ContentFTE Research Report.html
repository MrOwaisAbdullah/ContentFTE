COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE / WORDPRESS + CUSTOM LAUNCH

The market is crowded. The quality gate isn’t.

Soro is one competitor, not the category. Seven more products show where buyers now expect keyword discovery, internal links, schema, brand voice, calendars, native publishing, and visible search performance.

Launch decision

Start with custom sites and WordPress as planned. Keep the existing internal-linking and stock-image systems, then complete keyword discovery, metadata, schema, scheduling, and a thin WordPress plugin. Put ContentFTE’s 90+ gate, JEV judgments, and chat approvals at the center of the product.

Positioning

“Autonomous SEO publishing with a quality gate.” The category automates output. ContentFTE can own supervised autonomy: sources, revision history, site facts, approval in chat, and no silent publish below the configured threshold.

Choose the right battlefield

The largest review counts belong to Shopify SEO utilities, not autonomous blog systems. For a WordPress/custom launch, Koala, Autoblogging.ai, BlogSEO.ai and Abun are the product benchmarks; SEOWILL, StoreSEO and Jolt are feature and failure-pattern references.

The useful segmentation

Distribution suites: SEOWILL and StoreSEO win by bundling many merchant SEO chores and owning Shopify discovery.

Volume factories: Koala and Autoblogging.ai sell throughput, modes, publishing reach and integrations.

Workflow products: BlogSEO.ai and Abun connect keyword selection, production, scheduling and publishing. These are the closest launch comps.

Supervised autonomy: no researched competitor exposes messaging-native approve/reject plus a revision gate. That is ContentFTE’s wedge.

Scale estimates — directional only

Research estimates: SEOWILL ~$0.8–1.3M ARR; StoreSEO ~$0.4M ARR; Jolt ~$5–15k MRR; Koala ~$5–12M ARR; Autoblogging.ai ~$1–3M ARR; BlogSEO.ai and Abun each ~$0.5–0.7M ARR.

Do not treat these as audited revenue. They combine vendor user claims, review footprints, assumed paid conversion and assumed ARPU. Team, funding and exact customer counts were not public for several products.

Grounding must block publishing

Jolt’s phantom product and Soro’s inconsistent facts show why retrieval is not enough. Validate named products, claims, numbers and links against approved site data.

Credits create anxiety

SEOWILL, Koala and Autoblogging.ai complaints cluster around credits, multipliers or surprise charges. Prefer transparent post allowances with rollover and a visible per-job cost ledger.

Support is part of the product

Named, fast support drives strong reviews for the Shopify suites. Slow replies and refund disputes dominate negative narratives around larger writing tools.

Review quality matters more than count

StoreSEO review solicitation, BlogSEO’s paid-review allegation and Autoblogging’s affiliate-amplified ratings make organic, channel-native proof a strategic advantage.

Architecture for WordPress and custom sites

Move orchestration to PostgreSQL, but keep the first launch surface narrow: one thin WordPress plugin, one stable webhook/API contract for custom sites, and one shared content-job state machine.

1 · ProduceDeepSeek V4.1 Flash researches, briefs, drafts and revises.
→
2 · JudgeJEV handles structured micro-decisions; a frontier model performs the independent 90+ evaluation.
→
3 · DeliverHuman approval in chat, then WordPress plugin or custom-site SDK/API publishing.

WordPress delivery

Ship a wordpress.org plugin whose job is connection, health checks, draft/publish, featured image, canonical URL, categories/tags and SEO metadata sync. Confirm actual writes for Yoast and Rank Math; add FAQ + Article JSON-LD; prevent duplicate remote IDs.

DRAFT DEFAULT First-run sites should approve before publishing. Autopilot becomes an explicit site-level mode after a successful test post.

Custom-site delivery

Build a versioned SDK over the same API contract used by the WordPress connector. Include title, slug, HTML/Markdown body, meta title, description, schema, image assets, citations, internal-link targets and idempotency key. Require an acknowledgement and store the returned remote ID.

MCP COMES AFTER THE API Expose brief, draft, gate and publish tools only after create → update → rollback is stable.

PostgreSQL is the operational source of truth

Use PostgreSQL for tenants, jobs, retries, locks, schedules, approvals, provider spend and publication history. Keep Google Sheets only as optional import/export. Assign idempotency keys to generation and publishing; persist every transition as an event.

DataForSEO stays behind a budget boundary

Batch volume and difficulty lookups, cache by keyword + market + language, persist raw provider snapshots and expose normalized facts to agents. The model should not decide how many paid rows to fetch.

Guardrails: workspace spend cap, max rows/task, TTL, provider cost ledger and Tavily/SerpAPI fallback.

09

What to build, in order

The goal is not feature parity with seven products. It is a credible WordPress/custom launch with complete SEO basics, a visibly safer automation model, and one clean path from keyword to measured result.

P0 · Table stakes before launch

  1. Keyword discovery UIGSC striking-distance keywords + competitor gaps feeding triage. Per-post Tavily research does not answer “what should I write next?”
  2. Internal-link QA and backfillThe posting agent already retrieves internal links. Add reciprocal-link backfill, broken-target checks, anchor diversity and a report showing exactly what changed.
  3. Meta + schema validatorConfirm Yoast/Rank Math writes; emit FAQ + Article JSON-LD; block duplicate title/H1, missing description and invalid canonical.
  4. Calendar and job-status UIExpose queued → brief → draft → gated → scheduled → published, with drag/reschedule and per-post failure reason.
  5. Thin WordPress pluginConnection, health, content push, metadata, images and rollback. A wordpress.org listing buys trust and discovery.
  6. Four-language launch baselineStart with EN/ES/DE/FR if quality tests pass; competitors make multilingual support a standard buying criterion.

P1 · Differentiators to copy

  1. Brand profiles / “Brand DNA”Crawl once; store voice, audience, offers and approved facts for every brief.
  2. GEO / answer-engine visibilityllms.txt, FAQ schema and tracked brand citations in AI answers—after core SEO publishing works.
  3. Programmatic SEO page typesAbun’s comparison, glossary, calculator and statistics pages fit custom sites well.
  4. Custom-site SDK + MCP serverShip the SDK first for predictable integration; expose brief → draft → gate → publish tools through MCP once the API is stable.
  5. Flat post allowances + rolloverAvoid the category’s credit-multiplier and surprise-billing trap.

P2 · Advantages to market harder

Quality gate: show the rubric, failed checks and revision history. Messaging approvals: demo approve/reject in WhatsApp, Telegram and Discord. Research depth: expose the brief and source ledger. Economics: show $0.05–0.20/post and 15–17/day as supplied operating targets, with real telemetry replacing estimates after launch.

Explicitly do not copy

Backlink exchanges: delay until there is a defensible compliance and quality story. Confusing credit multipliers: keep one post equal to one allowance if credits are used. Review incentives: collect organic, channel-native proof. Silent autopublish: never treat generation success as permission to publish.

The launch story

“From keyword opportunity to approved WordPress draft—with evidence, automatic links, complete SEO fields, and a quality gate.” That is narrower than “AI SEO platform,” easier to demonstrate, and directly addresses the category’s most credible complaints.

Open questions to resolve in implementation: verify the current WordPress adapter’s Yoast/Rank Math writes; define what score 90 measures and which failures hard-block publishing; choose first multilingual markets; and replace supplied cost/throughput targets with observed telemetry.
